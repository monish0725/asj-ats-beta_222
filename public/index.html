What We Do

One workspace for the entire hiring lifecycle

From the moment a resume lands in your inbox to the day an offer is signed, ASJ ATS keeps recruiters, hiring managers, and account managers working from the same source of truth.

01

Parse & Analyze

Every resume is automatically parsed and run through a full ATS analysis — scoring, keyword coverage, and formatting checks — the moment it arrives.
